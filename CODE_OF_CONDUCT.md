# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Critique souls, not people. Assume competence and good faith, then ask.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any space where
you are representing this project.

## Expected

- **Review the artifact.** "This heuristic isn't falsifiable" is useful. "This soul is stupid" is not.
- **Say what you'd change.** A rejection without a path forward is just friction.
- **Accept a no.** A maintainer declining a soul is a judgment call about the registry, not a verdict on you.
- **Credit people.** If a soul is derived from someone's work, say so in the PR.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Sexual attention or advances
- Publishing someone's private information
- Submitting souls designed to deceive, harass, or harm end users
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or delete any contribution or comment that violates this
document, and may block accounts for repeated or severe violations. Decisions are made in
the interest of the registry and its users. Enforcement actions are not up for debate in
the thread where they happen; raise a concern privately with a maintainer instead.

## Reporting

Report a violation by opening a private report through GitHub's
[Report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) flow or by emailing the address in
`soul.json` of any soul authored by the maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially.
